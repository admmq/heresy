* Packages

Here I load packages I use.
#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  (defun stuff-packages-load-packages ()
    (use-package evil
      :config
      (evil-mode 1))
    (when (display-graphic-p)
      (use-package pdf-tools
        :config
        (pdf-loader-install))))
#+END_SRC

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  (provide 'stuff/packages)
#+END_SRC
